Knowledge Base · FAQs

Common questions about property advocacy.

Clear, honest answers about our fees, market selection, interstate buying, and SMSF rules.

Why do you physically visit the markets you recommend?

Data identifies macro-markets with strong numbers, but it can never reveal how individual pockets, micro-streets, and properties are perceived on the ground. Visiting an area and speaking with local residents, council workers, and agents lets us assess sentiment, street appeal, noise, and zoning factors that don't appear in spreadsheets. A suburb can look incredible on paper and still have streets you'd never want to own.

Can't desktop AI research and data identify the best suburbs alone?

Desktop data narrows down which suburbs deserve investigation — not which specific streets within them have high owner-occupier demand or which properties will lag in resale. That crucial second layer is where on-the-ground intelligence protects your capital.

How are you paid, and why is there $0 fee to the buyer?

We operate as property advocates with institutional developer and vendor agreements across Australia. Upon successful purchase and settlement, we are remunerated by the vendor. This allows everyday mum and dad investors and SMSF trustees to access professional research, due diligence, and negotiation with zero upfront fees.

Will you ever tell me NOT to buy a property?

Yes, absolutely. If on-the-ground validation reveals undisclosed red flags or if an asset does not stack up against your strategy, we will advise you against proceeding — even if that means concluding the call without a transaction.

Do you help with property purchases inside an SMSF?

Yes, both residential and commercial SMSF acquisitions. We handle the property sourcing and micro-market due diligence; your licensed accountant, financial adviser, and mortgage broker verify that the structure complies with ATO LRBA requirements.

I live in Sydney or Melbourne and want to buy interstate. How does that work?

The vast majority of our clients buy interstate. We conduct the macro-research, physically inspect the shortlisted streets, record video walkthroughs, and present comprehensive reports so you can make informed decisions without having to travel back and forth.

What happens on the first 15-minute strategy call?

Fifteen minutes, zero obligation, and zero properties pitched. We discuss your current financial position, goals, borrowing capacity, and timeframe, and provide clear strategic feedback on your next best step.
